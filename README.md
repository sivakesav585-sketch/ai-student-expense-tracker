AI Student Expense Tracker

About the Project

This project helps students track their daily expenses and manage their money.

Problem

Many students find it difficult to track their spending and save money.

Solution

Our app will help students record expenses, monitor their budget, and understand their spending habits.

Planned Features

- Add daily expenses
- View total monthly spending
- Track budget and savings
- Get AI-powered spending suggestions
